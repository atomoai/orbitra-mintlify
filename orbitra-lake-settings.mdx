# `orbitra.lake.settings`


The settings that only the lake SDK reads, on top of the platform's ``ClientSettings``.

## Classes

### `LakeClientSettings`


The platform settings plus the lake's own.

**Attributes:**
- `orbitra_lake_namespace_groups`: Namespace to the display names of its three Entra groups,
``{"reader"\: ..., "writer"\: ..., "manager"\: ...}``, as the tenant publishes them (the
``namespace_group_names`` output of the lake module). The row filters and column masks of
``TableSchemaWithPolicies`` need it. None when the tenant does not publish it.
