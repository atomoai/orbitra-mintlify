# `orbitra.lake.db`


Orbitra db module: the storage backends of the lake.

- ``base.py`` is the contract (``BaseDB``).
- ``factory.py`` picks a backend.
- ``local.py`` and ``pyiceberg.py`` implement it.
- ``unity/`` is the Unity backend: ``UnityDB`` in its ``__init__``; ``_rest.py`` is the Unity Catalog REST
  transport (the token per call, ``redact()``, retries), and ``_policies.py`` is the policy part, mixed
  into ``UnityDB``.

The helper modules of a backend live inside that backend's subpackage, with a leading underscore
(internal: no compatibility promise). The factory chooses only backends.
